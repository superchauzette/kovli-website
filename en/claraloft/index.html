Where she's from

Nobody really knows. She says São Paulo one night, Mexico the next — and no one corrects her.

What's certain: she walked into the Paris night the way you walk in without knocking, and nothing sounded the same after. She learned music in kitchens, back rooms, parties that never ended — the kind of schooling no diploma can hold.

She brings the heat, the movement, something a little out of control. One note higher, one step sideways, and a whole track tips over.

KØVLI & CLARA

Two poles. One same night.

We met them the same winter, in the same basement. Since then, no one can picture them apart.

Where KØVLI drifts, she sets things alight. He cools, she warms. Together they make the tension, the release, and everything in between.

What ties them

“No one ever knew. Maybe not even them.”

— and that's exactly how they like it

Best friends. Partners in crime. Something else. We've seen them leave together at dawn. We've seen them ignore each other for weeks. What connects them has no name — and they prefer it that way. The rest is nobody's business.
